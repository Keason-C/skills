---

Review the spec. Return review findings by default; modify files only when explicitly requested.

1. **Understand the business.** Read the spec and the project's business background or mission, using the paths supplied by the user or project instructions, or `docs/mission.md` by default. Understand the target users, their needs, and the project's goals and current scope.

2. **Check the scope.** Review the spec against current business needs for excessive design or scope beyond the intended users and goals.

3. **Share the review.** Explain any issues and recommendations in the context of the project's business needs.
